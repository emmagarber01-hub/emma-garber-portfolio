I'm Emma Garber, a Los Angeles–based stylist and creative working across celebrity, editorial, campaign and performance. I'm drawn to the tension between polish and personality — clothes with a point of view, images with a pulse, and stories that feel unmistakably individual.

My work moves between styling, creative direction, fashion storytelling and digital content. From the first reference to the final look, I bring a perspective rooted in curiosity, collaboration and a little bit of beautiful disruption.
