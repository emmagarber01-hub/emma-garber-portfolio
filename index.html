I'm Emma Garber, a Los Angeles–based fashion stylist and creative working across celebrity, editorial, campaign, runway and performance. My work moves between wardrobe, image-making and visual storytelling — building looks and worlds with a distinct point of view.

From celebrity dressing and editorial concepts to music videos, fashion campaigns and independent projects, I approach each collaboration through character, silhouette, texture and a strong visual instinct.
